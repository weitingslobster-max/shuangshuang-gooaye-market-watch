Theme analysis

1. PLTR: AI-software risk appetite is officially stronger, but price discipline matters more

Palantir’s Q2 2026 official release was stronger than the prior open trigger required. Revenue grew 93% YoY to $1.935B; U.S. commercial revenue grew 149% YoY to $764M; GAAP operating margin was 47%; adjusted FCF margin was 63%; FY2026 revenue guidance was raised to $8.150B-$8.158B. This validates the “AI software risk-appetite signal” from EP685.

The conclusion is not “buy anything at any price.” It is: PLTR’s business proof improved enough to keep it at the top of the AI-software watchlist, but valuation, contract timing, government/commercial mix, and implied growth duration must be tested before position sizing.

2. AMD: Q2 improved the rack-scale AI / CPU-platform lane

AMD’s Q2 release supports the EP681/EP685 rack-scale AI and CPU-platform hypotheses. Revenue reached $11.5B, Data Center revenue was $6.7B and up 107% YoY, and management guided Q3 revenue to about $13B plus/minus $300M. Official highlights included Helios rack-scale solutions, Instinct MI400/MI455X, 6th Gen EPYC, ROCm.ai, Anthropic collaboration up to 2GW of MI450 GPUs in Helios racks, and Azure Helios deployment plans.

That is a meaningful evidence upgrade. The next gate is execution: Q3 revenue/margin delivery, actual customer deployment conversion, software maturity, and whether Instinct/EPYC demand translates into durable operating leverage.

3. AVGO: still the strongest custom-AI proxy, but no new action today

Broadcom’s Q2 FY2026 release remains the cleanest primary-source custom-AI evidence in this dashboard: Q2 revenue $22.187B, semiconductor AI revenue $10.8B up 143% YoY, and Q3 AI semiconductor revenue expected at $16.0B, up over 200% YoY. That keeps AVGO as priority research and a benchmark for MRVL/GOOGL/TSM read-throughs.

The discipline is unchanged: high thesis quality does not remove customer-concentration and valuation risk.

4. TSM: next hard data point is still August 10 Taiwan time

TSMC’s June revenue was already strong: NT$442.68B, up 67.9% YoY, with January-June revenue up 35.6% YoY. July monthly sales are scheduled for 2026-08-10 at 13:30 Asia/Taipei. Until then, TSM remains the AI-hardware anchor, but no fresh upgrade is justified today.

5. Optical / LITE: wait for August 11 rather than chase the narrative

Lumentum’s official IR calendar says FY2026 Q4 and full-year results will be released on 2026-08-11 after the market closes. Keep LITE/COHR/MRVL/NOK in high-beta monitor status. Upgrade only if revenue, AI datacenter demand, margins and customer concentration all look acceptable.
