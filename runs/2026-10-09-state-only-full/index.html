Theme analysis

1) EP703 is already incorporated: avoid duplicate-signal bias

The most important process decision today is to not treat EP703 as a new signal again. Reusing yesterday's episode as if it were fresh would overweight one input and create false confidence. The correct state-only action is to preserve the message while demanding new proof from official sources.

The active translation remains: no leverage, no automatic dip-buying, no FOMO basket, preserve cash as option value, and cut weak/non-confirming names first.

2) TSM: still the anchor, but Q3 call is the real proof gate

TSM is still the cleanest U.S.-tradable expression of the Taiwan EPS/HPC anchor. However, today's decision should not depend on anecdote or price momentum. The next real test is management's Q3 commentary: HPC/AI mix, pricing discipline, overseas-fab cost drag, capex ROI, and 2027 visibility.

Until that call clears, the right posture is 核心觀察 / core watch: maintain disciplined exposure if already owned, but do not upgrade solely because the broader AI tape is strong.

3) MRVL/AVGO/optical: the thesis is valid, but the burden of proof is higher

Marvell's official Investor Day material keeps the custom silicon, scale-up networking, and optical-interconnect lane relevant. That is a real reason to keep MRVL and related optical names in priority research. It is not a reason to buy every optical-adjacent stock.

Upgrade conditions should stay strict: bookings become revenue, revenue becomes margin, margin becomes FCF, and customer concentration does not create a hidden cliff. MXL, LITE, and COHR remain high-beta monitors rather than core holdings.

4) Cost-relief beneficiaries: useful screen, but no upgrade yet

The new EP703-derived screen is still interesting: companies hurt by memory, CPU, component, freight, or availability pressure may benefit if supply normalizes. But cost relief only creates shareholder value when demand holds. If lower input costs are paired with weak end demand, the screen becomes a value trap.

For now, DELL/HPQ/HPE/CSCO/GLW/SYNA/LOGI are research candidates. The next upgrade needs management language in official filings/calls that confirms both lower costs and resilient orders or shipments.

5) NeoCloud/high-beta stories: keep them outside the core

CRWV/NBIS-type names are still useful as risk-case monitors for AI infrastructure financing, utilization, and capex intensity. They should not become core exposures without evidence on contract quality, depreciation, funding cost, and free-cash-flow trajectory. EP703's 靠賽 psychology examples are not portfolio construction rules.
