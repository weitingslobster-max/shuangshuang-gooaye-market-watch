Theme analysis

1) OCP 2026 confirms the lane, not the trade

Broadcom's official OCP release says it will present scale-up, scale-out and scale-across AI networking innovations for OCP racks, including Ethernet switches, NICs, PCIe components and integrated optics. This is directly relevant to the EP704 interconnect thesis. It supports the idea that the bottleneck is shifting beyond GPUs into networking, optical links, rack architecture and system integration.

But the investment conclusion stays disciplined: OCP is mostly roadmap / product evidence. It does not by itself prove incremental revenue, margins, customer concentration risk, working-capital quality or free-cash-flow conversion.

2) AVGO remains the cleanest liquid comparator

AVGO has the strongest official evidence set among the U.S.-tradable names: production-scale switching, OCP AI networking visibility, custom ASIC/networking leadership and broad software cash-flow support. That makes it the clean comparator for the whole lane.

The stock discipline is unchanged: do not chase valuation just because the business narrative is high quality. Upgrade only if new filings or earnings show AI networking/custom silicon growth with durable free cash flow and no financing-quality deterioration.

3) MRVL stays important, but proof burden is higher after Analyst Day

MRVL remains the key priority-research name because it sits closer to the DSP / optical / custom-silicon thesis that EP704 emphasized. However, after a roadmap-heavy event cycle, the standard rises. The next evidence should be:

  • recognized AI/custom-silicon revenue, not only pipeline;
  • 800G/1.6T traction that is visible in gross margin;
  • customer diversification beyond a narrow hyperscaler base;
  • free-cash-flow conversion and working-capital discipline.

Until then, MRVL is priority research / no chase, not a core position upgrade.

4) TSM Q3 is the hard gate

TSM is still the cleanest U.S.-tradable expression for foundry/HPC demand through the ADR. The Q3 call on 2026-10-15 should test the most important open questions: gross margin, overseas fab costs, AI/HPC pricing, CoWoS/advanced packaging visibility, capex, and management's 2027 demand confidence.

The correct pre-call stance is patience. Do not add exposure ahead of the call purely because OCP networking news is supportive.
