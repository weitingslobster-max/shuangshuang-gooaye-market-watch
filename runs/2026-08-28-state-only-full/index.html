Theme analysis

1. MRVL: proof improved, but now the test becomes quality of growth

Marvell’s Q2 FY27 release directly improved yesterday’s biggest open gate. Revenue was above the prior midpoint, Data Center was 79% of total revenue, and Q3 revenue guidance implies another strong sequential step. This is enough to move the MRVL thesis from “headline/warrant-only risk” to “fundamental proof improved.”

The caution is that stronger growth also increases concentration. The Google agreement and warrant align incentives, but investors still need to monitor dilution, customer power, margin mix, and whether custom silicon contributes high-quality earnings rather than lower-margin service-like revenue.

Investment read: upgrade evidence quality, not risk appetite. MRVL deserves priority research, but chasing immediately after the proof event is still a poor process.

2. GOOGL: still the TPU ROI firewall

If MRVL and AVGO are supplier-side beneficiaries, Alphabet is the customer-side proof gate. The market-watch framework should keep asking whether Cloud and AI revenue convert into operating income and free cash flow after capex, depreciation, leases and purchase obligations. Supplier revenue visibility is more durable when GOOGL’s Cloud economics remain healthy.

Investment read: GOOGL remains core watch. MRVL proof is bullish for the TPU ecosystem, but GOOGL economics determine how much supplier visibility is sustainable.

3. AVGO: COT is not a downgrade, it is a margin durability question

Broadcom still has a stronger cash-conversion and incumbent custom-ASIC/networking profile than most peers. EP690’s COT framing should not be simplified into “MRVL wins, AVGO loses.” The real question is whether hyperscalers can modularize enough of custom AI procurement to pressure turnkey margin pools.

Investment read: keep AVGO priority research. Upgrade if AI semiconductor growth, backlog quality and margins stay excellent; downgrade only if COT pressure shows up in margin/customer-concentration evidence.

4. NVDA and TSM: anchor proof remains intact, valuation discipline becomes more important

NVIDIA’s Q2 FY27 proof from yesterday remains the strongest evidence for AI infrastructure demand. TSM remains the clean liquid ADR anchor for foundry/advanced-packaging strength. Neither should be used to justify chasing every downstream supplier. The better use is as a confidence anchor for staged entries when price provides a reasonable risk/reward.

5. AI power, memory and OSAT: second-derivative lanes remain proof-gated

ETN/VRT, MU, AMKR/ASX and related power/packaging names remain active because MRVL/NVDA strength supports the broader infrastructure cycle. But their stocks should be judged on their own order conversion, margin expansion, capex burden and FCF, not merely on association with AI.

Valuation and entry discipline

  • Core watch: NVDA, TSM, GOOGL, ETN, SMH/SOXX.
  • Priority research: MRVL, AVGO, AMD, MU, VRT, AMKR, ASX, PLTR.
  • Wait for better entry: MRVL after earnings proof, NVDA after Q2 proof, high-beta power/optical/OSAT names.
  • Small satellite only: thin OTC ADR expressions such as MRAAY only if broker-level liquidity/spread is acceptable.
  • Avoid: local-only Taiwan/Japan/Korea tickers, headline-only COT/TPU trades, and leverage.
