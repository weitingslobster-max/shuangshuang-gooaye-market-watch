Theme analysis

1. Custom silicon and NVIDIA architecture can coexist

EP693's best frame remains intact: custom ASIC/XPU demand does not automatically mean NVIDIA loses the cycle. NVIDIA is trying to make custom accelerators plug into NVLink Fusion, NVHBM, MGX, networking, and software. That turns the investment question from “GPU versus ASIC” into “who captures system-level economics?”

US-tradable expressions remain NVDA, AVGO, MRVL, TSM, MU, and semi ETFs. MediaTek/Alchip-type local tickers stay signals, not direct US recommendations.

2. AVGO: demand proof improved; FOMO risk also increased

Broadcom's official Q3 FY26 release remains the strongest new evidence from the EP693 cycle. The numbers validate custom AI semiconductor demand and networking leverage, but they also raise the hurdle for incremental returns. For the watchlist, this means:

  • treat AVGO as a higher-quality thesis than before the print;
  • do not convert “strong quarter” into “buy at any price”;
  • watch 10-Q/call details for concentration, gross margin, operating margin, free cash flow, and VMware debt/cash-flow contribution.

3. MRVL: high-priority, but still needs economics proof

Marvell remains the best proof lane for custom silicon plus optical/interconnect outside Broadcom. The next upgrade requires evidence that AI-related growth converts into margin quality and cash generation. Until then, MRVL should be monitored aggressively but sized cautiously.

4. TSM and advanced packaging

TSM is still the cleanest US-tradable manufacturing anchor for AI custom silicon, HBM base-die, and advanced packaging. However, today's run has no new official August monthly revenue datapoint, so the conclusion is unchanged: keep TSM as a core watch and wait for the official monthly revenue update before declaring another proof gate cleared.

5. MU / HBM / NVHBM

NVHBM keeps memory economics on the active dashboard. The upside case is that bandwidth and packaging complexity sustain HBM demand. The risk case is that NVIDIA/system architects capture more of the value and memory vendors face tougher bargaining dynamics. MU remains a useful US-tradable monitor, but it is cyclical and entry-sensitive.

6. Hyperscaler ROI firewall

GOOGL, MSFT, AMZN, META and other hyperscalers are the buyers behind the infrastructure cycle. For this report's active table, GOOGL remains the key TPU/AI capex ROI monitor. If cloud operating income and AI monetization do not absorb capex, the whole AI infrastructure chain becomes more vulnerable.
