Trigger-focused analysis

1) AI security / compliance: improving proof, still not a blank check

CrowdStrike’s latest official results reported record net new ARR of $333 million, ending ARR of $5.84 billion, and expanded Falcon AIDR integrations across multiple AI gateway partners. Palo Alto’s FY2026 Q4 results reported Next-Generation Security ARR of $9.10 billion and RPO of $21.2 billion.

This is enough to keep CRWD and PANW as priority research for EP697’s AI security / compliance lane. It is not enough to call the AI-security theme “fully monetized.” The next upgrade needs more direct evidence: AI-security attach, AIDR or Cortex/Idira adoption, regulated-enterprise wins, renewal impact, or management quantification.

Action: keep CRWD/PANW on watch; use CIBR for diversified exposure if single-name valuation is uncomfortable.

2) AI infrastructure: official demand is strong, but concentration risk is rising

Broadcom’s Q3 FY2026 release showed very strong AI semiconductor revenue growth and high free cash flow conversion. NVIDIA’s Q2 FY2027 release showed data-center revenue growth still driving the model. TSMC’s August 2026 monthly revenue rose strongly year over year and month over month.

That keeps the AI infrastructure stack constructive: AVGO / NVDA / TSM / MRVL / MU remain important. But the report should not become a “buy anything AI” engine. NVIDIA’s 10-Q also makes the infrastructure buildout more financialized: land, power, shell, capital availability and financing platforms are becoming part of the investment risk.

Action: separate business quality from stock entry. Upgrade only when demand converts into margin, FCF and customer breadth; downgrade if financing structures, customer concentration or capex returns deteriorate.

3) Cloud ROI firewall: still the core sanity check

Microsoft’s FY2026 Q4 materials show strong cloud and AI demand, but also remind us that cloud AI growth requires heavy infrastructure investment and lease/capex discipline. This is the cross-check for the entire AI supply chain: supplier revenue is stronger when cloud customers can convert AI usage into operating income and free cash flow.

Action: treat hyperscaler capex as productive only when paired with revenue, utilization, operating income and FCF evidence. ORCL remains the sharpest financing-quality watch; MSFT/GOOGL/AMZN/META remain the broader ROI firewall.
