Material change: AMKR cleared the first operating proof gate

Amkor’s Q2 release is a meaningful positive for the Gooaye OSAT / advanced-packaging lane. The prior state said strategic NVIDIA and TSMC partnership announcements were not enough by themselves. The July 27 Q2 numbers now provide the first operating confirmation: record Q2 sales, large year-over-year growth, margin expansion, and stronger Q3 guide.

The important nuance: advanced packaging is capital intensive. A higher revenue and margin guide is good, but the investment case still needs evidence that the added capex produces durable returns rather than only one or two strong quarters. For AMKR, upgrade the evidence quality, not the risk tolerance.

Mature-semi recovery breadth

TXN remains the cleanest mature-semi recovery proof in state. STM adds partial support with Q2 revenue of $3.49B and a Q3 midpoint guide of $3.70B revenue / 37.0% gross margin. Together with AMKR’s improvement, the recovery breadth looks better than it did yesterday.

Still, the proof chain is incomplete. ON, ADI, VSH, ASX, and UMC need to confirm orders, utilization, pricing/mix, margins, and cash conversion. If the next companies show restocking without sustainable margin, avoid upgrading the whole group.
