Theme analysis

1) EP701 turns Jalapeño from background proof into a main research lane

OpenAI and Broadcom's official Jalapeño materials support the core EP701 point. The program disclosed a nine-month tape-out, OpenAI-designed inference accelerator architecture, Broadcom implementation/networking, and Celestica rack/system participation. The useful investment question is not whether every IC designer suddenly becomes more valuable; it is whether AI-assisted design and custom silicon shorten cycles enough to raise demand for EDA tools, implementation partners, networking silicon, packaging/rack integration, and specialist design talent.

US-tradable read-through: AVGO remains the cleanest named partner. SNPS/CDNS become more interesting as picks-and-shovels if AI-assisted workflows increase simulation, verification, IP reuse, and design iteration. MRVL remains a custom-silicon/optical proof gate. SMH/SOXX are fallback expressions when single-name conviction is low.

2) Small IC-design spillover is real as a hypothesis, but weak as a US-tradable expression

EP701's host view says large IC-design houses may prioritize AI/TPU/custom silicon work, allowing second-tier design houses to receive non-core orders. That is plausible, but the direct beneficiaries are mostly Taiwan-local and company-specific. Within this workflow's US scope, the right move is to track the signal, not invent a forced ADR trade.

Discipline: do not import Taiwan local tickers into the US watchlist unless there is a clean US-listed ADR/ETF/proxy with acceptable liquidity. Use the theme to ask better questions of AVGO/MRVL/QCOM/AMD/SNPS/CDNS, not to chase thin OTCs.

3) Micron cleared the memory proof gate — but price still matters

MU's official FY2026 Q4 was the most important new primary-source event since the prior run. Revenue was $54.229B, GAAP gross margin was 86.8%, non-GAAP EPS was $33.42, and adjusted free cash flow was $33.20B for the quarter. Guidance for FQ1-27 revenue was $61.5B ± $1.5B with roughly 86% gross margin. Product highlights included high-capacity DDR5 RDIMMs, server SOCAMM revenue more than doubling sequentially, KV-cache SSD products, AI-workstation design wins with every Tier 1 OEM, and LPDDR6 sampling.

Investment judgment: upgrade MU to 核心觀察 / core watch. The result validates HBM/memory capacity as an AI bottleneck. But no automatic chase: memory is cyclical, capex is rising, expectations are now high, and supply responses can eventually compress margins.

4) TSMC Texas / Dallas is a rumor until official sources confirm it

EP701 treats TSMC Texas/Dallas as a high-impact but early/rumor item. Official TSMC materials currently support Arizona/U.S. expansion, including Arizona production and additional U.S. fabs/advanced packaging/R&D planning; they do not confirm a Dallas/Texas site. The official near-term TSM gates remain September monthly sales on 2026-10-08 Taipei time and Q3 results/call on 2026-10-15.

US-tradable read-through: TSM remains the core liquid expression of foundry/HPC strength. U.S. construction/facility/material beneficiaries should not be upgraded until there is official site selection, incentive filing, permitting, or supplier order evidence.

5) Separate host view from secondary-note extensions

EP701's local notes explicitly separate host view from Social Worker Daily author interpretation. The workflow therefore excludes 國巨, 3661, 群聯 and Tesla Roadster from the US-tradable investment watchlist.
