Theme analysis

1. Oracle / AI cloud: demand proof improved, balance-sheet proof still pending

Oracle is now the most important near-term monitor because the primary-source data are both bullish and cautionary. OCI demand and RPO are large enough to matter for the whole AI infrastructure chain. But the stock thesis cannot stop at RPO: the company is funding an aggressive cloud buildout, and the investment test is whether the booked demand converts into durable revenue, margin, free cash flow and balance-sheet flexibility.

Upgrade path: RPO converts into recognized revenue and operating profit while capex intensity normalizes, customer concentration is manageable, and funding sources become less dilutive/levered. Downgrade path: RPO is too long-duration or concentrated; customer prepayments, leases, debt, or equity issuance become a recurring funding requirement; free cash flow remains structurally negative.

2. Broadcom / custom AI: currently cleaner cash economics, but watch concentration

Broadcom's Q3 FY26 release gives better cash-quality evidence than most AI-infrastructure narratives: large AI semiconductor revenue, high operating income, and free cash flow equal to roughly 46% of revenue. That keeps AVGO near the top of the quality watchlist.

But because custom accelerators can concentrate around a few hyperscale customers, AVGO still needs concentration/backstop scrutiny. Strong FCF deserves respect; it does not remove valuation risk.

3. TSM / foundry anchor: revenue proof strong; margin proof next

TSMC's August monthly revenue strengthened the AI-stack dashboard. The ADR remains a clean US-tradable expression of foundry and advanced packaging strength. The next question is not whether AI demand exists; it is whether Q3 commentary confirms margin durability despite overseas fab costs, customer mix and capacity expansion.

4. MRVL / custom silicon and optical: wait for Investor Day

Marvell's Q2 FY27 result improved the data-center/custom silicon evidence, but the key proof gate is now Investor Day on 2026-10-06. The report should quantify custom ramp, optical attach, margin trajectory, free cash flow, concentration and any preferred-equity/dilution implications.

5. MU / memory and local AI read-through

Micron remains the clean US-tradable monitor for HBM/NAND/storage economics, including any eventual local/private AI storage read-through from Phison-like architectures. The Sep. 30 report is the right confirmation point. Until then, avoid over-reading NAND/local-AI anecdotes.

6. Apple iPhone Duo: product optionality, not supplier FOMO

Apple's Q3 FY26 showed strong company-level revenue and gross margin, which supports AAPL as the clean product-cycle vehicle. But the foldable/iPhone Duo thesis still requires launch evidence: lead times, reviews, durability, app continuity and gross-margin commentary. Local hinge/component names remain signals only unless a liquid US expression exists.
