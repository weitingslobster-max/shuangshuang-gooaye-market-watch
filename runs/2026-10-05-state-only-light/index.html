TSM remains the cleanest liquid U.S.-tradable foundry expression through the ADR. The next proof should come from monthly sales and the Q3 earnings call, especially HPC demand, overseas manufacturing cost, gross-margin discipline and capacity/pricing comments.

3) Passive / power shortage lane

The shortage thesis remains plausible but not yet proven enough for broad upgrades. VSH and MRAAY/MRAAF are imperfect passive expressions; MPWR/ON/ADI/TXN/STM/UMC are more liquid but often less pure. Upgrade only when official results show AI/server-specific revenue, bookings, pricing, lead-time or margin conversion.

4) AI cost governance

Keep AI cost governance as a risk overlay, not a bearish standalone trade. If enterprises shift toward private/on-prem AI or tighter API usage controls, the winners may be infrastructure suppliers, efficient platforms, and software vendors that can monetize AI without destroying margins.
