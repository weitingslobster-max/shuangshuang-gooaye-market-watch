Theme analysis

1. TSMC July revenue clears the most important open gate

Yesterday’s state left TSMC July revenue open because the run could not primary-verify it. Today, TSMC’s own monthly revenue page shows July 2026 revenue of NT$467.580B and +44.7% YoY. That is a real positive data point for the AI/HPC anchor lane and supports keeping TSM / SMH / SOXX as core research expressions.

Discipline: this is confirmation, not a blank-check buy signal. The next question is whether Q3 monthly revenue, gross margin, capex, and overseas fab cost commentary keep supporting the valuation.

2. AI data-center power has better primary-source support than yesterday

Vertiv’s Q2 release reported Q2 net sales of $3.274B, up 24% YoY, raised full-year guidance, and explicitly framed the data-center market as still showing strong momentum. MPS’s latest available 2026 commentary showed Enterprise Data revenue almost doubling YoY, with AI/server power-management solutions as the driver. Eaton is less pure, but its Electrical Americas order/backlog commentary and Boyd Thermal acquisition make it a higher-quality diversified expression.

Investment translation: VRT is the purest power/thermal infrastructure proxy, ETN is steadier but less pure, and MPWR is the stronger silicon-level power-management expression. Keep all three on priority research, but do not chase them without valuation work.

3. Optical should wait for Lumentum after the close

Lumentum has not reported FY2026 Q4 yet at this run time. The official IR release says the result will come after the market closes on 2026-08-11 with a 2:00pm PT webcast. That means LITE, COHR, MRVL, and NOK remain active monitors, not upgraded conclusions.

Upgrade only if Lumentum shows AI datacenter demand converting into revenue, margin, free cash flow, and credible forward guidance. Revenue-only excitement is not enough.

4. PLTR / NET remain useful tape signals, not automatic buys

The state already treats PLTR and NET as risk-appetite markers. That remains right. Strong expensive software can tell us that the market is willing to pay for AI growth again, but it does not solve valuation risk. Any portfolio action should be based on primary filings, revenue durability, customer concentration, profitability and free cash flow, not price strength alone.
