Theme analysis

1) Marvell / optical-DSP / custom-silicon event gate

Marvell's official Q2 FY2027 release supports the broad direction: record revenue, data-center growth, and AI-related demand across data-center products. The 10-Q language also matters because it broadens the demand driver beyond one sub-bucket: electro-optics, custom, storage and switching all contributed. That is constructive for EP702's optical/core-chain thesis.

But the investment question is stricter: does Investor Day turn the story into durable long-term economics? For an upgrade, the event should provide at least some combination of multi-year revenue visibility, customer breadth, optical/DSP attach, custom-silicon margins, operating leverage and free-cash-flow discipline. If the event stays mainly qualitative, MRVL remains watchlist priority but not upgraded.

2) TSMC / HPC foundry anchor

TSM remains the cleanest liquid U.S.-tradable foundry expression. TSMC's official Q3 guidance is strong: revenue guidance of US$44.6B–45.8B and gross-margin guidance of 65.0%–67.0%. January–August monthly revenue was also strong, but September data was still blank at run time. The correct discipline is to wait for the September monthly revenue release and the October 15 call before making a new label change.

3) MXL / 800G–1.6T optical DSP proof gate

MaxLinear is more levered and higher risk than MRVL/AVGO. Official Q2 commentary points to rapid data-center-oriented infrastructure growth and Keystone PAM4 DSP momentum for 800G, with a 1.6T roadmap. That makes MXL a clean optical-DSP proof candidate, but the next decision belongs after Q3 results: revenue growth must show margin and cash-quality conversion, not just roadmap excitement.

4) Passive / power and AI-server shortage lane

No new primary-source price-letter or allocation evidence was found in this run. Keep VSH, MPWR, ON, ADI, TXN, STM, UMC and MRAAY/MRAAF proof-gated. The shortage thesis can be right in the industry but still fail as a U.S.-tradable investment idea if the economics do not appear in reported bookings, margins, lead times or free cash flow.

5) AI cost governance

AI cost governance remains a market-wide risk overlay. If enterprise AI usage shifts toward API discipline, private deployments or stricter workload controls, the suppliers that win should show actual paid usage and margin absorption. Watch MSFT/GOOGL/AMZN/META/ORCL and enterprise software names for whether AI revenue can outrun capex, depreciation and inference cost.
