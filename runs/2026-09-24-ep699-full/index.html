Theme analysis

1) EP699 agentic AI → CPU demand: useful, but must be de-hyped

EP699's CPU discussion is timely because there is now official source support for the broader idea:

  • Meta officially introduced Muse as a personal AI agent, making consumer-facing agent workflows more concrete.
  • AMD's official EPYC agentic-AI materials argue that CPU infrastructure is important for agent sandboxes, GPU host-node work, general-purpose orchestration, memory capacity, I/O, and enterprise workloads.
  • AMD Q2 2026 already showed Data Center revenue up 107% year over year, driven by EPYC and Instinct demand.

Investment interpretation: this supports AMD as a priority research name and META as an adoption signal. It does not support treating extreme CPU/GPU ratios as base case. The report keeps the 40:1-style claim in the risk bucket, not the model.

2) DSP / optical interconnect: MRVL upgraded to a clearer proof-gate; MXL added cautiously

EP699's most actionable new industry clue is DSP/optical interconnect.

  • Marvell's official materials list 1.6T PAM4/coherent/AEC DSP products and broader interconnect capability.
  • MaxLinear's official PAM4 DSP page covers 100G, 400G, 800G, and 1.6T optical interconnects; its annual-report language says the Rushmore 1.6T DSP platform was expected to enter production later in 2026 with revenue ramping in 2027.
  • Marvell's Q2 FY2027 10-Q says revenue was $2.7B, up 37% YoY, with data-center sales up 46% from strong AI-related demand.

Investment interpretation: MRVL remains the better liquid US-tradable expression because it combines data-center scale, custom silicon, electro-optics, and AI networking. MXL is a valid watch item but should stay small/high-risk because scale, product ramp timing, customer concentration, and liquidity/volatility are more demanding.

3) AI infrastructure proof still supports the existing dashboard

Primary-source evidence remains constructive:

  • TSMC August 2026 revenue was approximately NT$514.81B, up 53.3% YoY; January-August revenue was up 39.3% YoY.
  • NVIDIA Q2 FY2027 revenue was $96.2B, up 106% YoY; Data Center revenue was $89.0B, up 117% YoY.
  • AMD Q2 2026 revenue was $11.5B, up 50% YoY; Data Center revenue was $6.7B, up 107% YoY.
  • Broadcom Q3 FY2026 AI semiconductor revenue was $16.7B, up 221% YoY and 54% QoQ.
  • Marvell Q2 FY2027 revenue was $2.7B, up 37% YoY, with data-center sales up 46%.
  • Micron Q3 FY2026 results kept HBM/memory constructive, but Q4 on 2026-09-30 is the next hard gate.
  • Vertiv Q2 2026 net sales grew 24% YoY, adjusted operating margin reached 22.6%, and guidance was raised.
  • Eaton Q2 Electrical Americas sales reached a record $4.0B, with rolling twelve-month Electrical Americas orders up 41% organically and backlog up 33% YoY.

Investment implication: demand evidence is not broken. The hard questions are valuation, margin capture, customer concentration, cloud ROI, and whether spec/cost-down pressure shifts value away from upstream suppliers.

4) AI security remains measurable, not automatic

CRWD and PANW are not central to EP699, but they remain useful because agentic AI can increase security/compliance demand:

  • CrowdStrike Q2 FY2027 ARR grew 25% YoY to $5.84B, with $332.8M net new ARR.
  • Palo Alto Networks Q4 FY2026 NGS ARR grew 63% YoY to $9.10B.

Stance: keep CRWD/PANW/CIBR in priority research. Do not chase solely because AI-security language is attractive.

Valuation and entry discipline

  • No leverage and no FOMO. EP699 is explicitly useful because it warns against exaggerated agentic-AI math.
  • Agent adoption is not the same as revenue. Muse-like apps need DAU/retention, monetization, privacy safety, and infrastructure-spend translation.
  • DSP product exposure is not the same as pricing power. Require MRVL/MXL management commentary on ASP, backlog, production ramps, gross margin, and CPO/LPO transition.
  • Prefer staged exposure or ETFs when the thesis is broad but the best single-name entry is unclear.
