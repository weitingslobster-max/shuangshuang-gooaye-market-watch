Theme analysis

AI ROI: MSFT versus META

MSFT is currently the cleaner proof chain: cloud growth, Azure acceleration, Copilot seat disclosure, and positive earnings power all point in the same direction. It does not make MSFT cheap automatically, but it strengthens the benchmark for what acceptable AI capex ROI should look like.

META is the opposite control case. Q2 ad and engagement data were strong, but the capex/expense profile means the stock should not be treated as a simple AI winner without FCF and depreciation discipline. The bar for upgrading META is formal monetization proof and sustained free-cash-flow recovery.

OSAT / advanced packaging

AMKR delivered the best fresh incremental signal. Revenue, gross margin, and Q3 guide all improved, and management tied the improvement to computing, automotive/industrial, AI/HPC programs, and advanced packaging/test capacity. This supports the EP676-EP678 OSAT thesis, but it is not a blanket chase signal because capex remains high and customer concentration is always a packaging-chain risk.

AMD Helios / Venice lane

AMD’s upcoming Q2 result is now the near-term catalyst. Official AMD IR items continue to support customer momentum around MI450/Helios/Venice, including Microsoft and Anthropic-related announcements, but the investment gate is the Q2 result plus margin, supply, ROCm/customer breadth, and 2027 visibility. The prior warning remains: do not convert the CPU-platform thesis into a simplistic near-term CPU:GPU ratio assumption.
