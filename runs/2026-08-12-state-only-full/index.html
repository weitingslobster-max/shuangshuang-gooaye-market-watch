Theme analysis

1. Lumentum turns the optical lane from narrative to proof

The prior run correctly waited for the Lumentum after-close result. The official release now provides real evidence: Q4 revenue doubled YoY, margins improved sharply, and Q1 FY2027 guidance stepped up materially from the Q4 base. Management’s language was directly tied to AI datacenter architecture: 1.6T cloud module adoption, CPO lasers, ELS modules, and NPO engagements.

Translation: LITE becomes a primary proof name for optical AI datacenter demand. COHR and MRVL remain peer/read-through beneficiaries, but they still need their own margin, customer and guide proof. NOK stays a lower-purity optical-network turnaround monitor.

Discipline: the result is strong enough to upgrade research priority, not strong enough to ignore valuation. Optical names can gap on guidance and reverse on customer concentration, export policy or telecom weakness.

2. AI infrastructure breadth is improving

Lumentum’s optical result fits the broader state thesis: AI spend is broadening from GPUs/foundry into power, thermal, optics, networking, packaging and power management. That supports keeping VRT, ETN, MPWR, MRVL, AVGO, AMKR, ASX, TSM, SMH and SOXX on the active dashboard.

The highest-conviction implementation remains liquid and staged. Use ETFs when single-name valuations are stretched or when the evidence is theme-wide but not company-specific.

3. TSMC remains the core anchor, but not a chase

Yesterday’s TSMC July revenue gate was already cleared-positive: NT$467.580B July revenue, +44.7% YoY, and YTD revenue +37.0%. Nothing today weakens that conclusion. The next proof points are August monthly revenue, Q3 margin/capex commentary and whether AI infrastructure strength continues without valuation compression.
