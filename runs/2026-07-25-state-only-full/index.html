GOOGL and META are the cleanest large-cap tests for whether AI infrastructure spending is becoming profitable usage rather than only supplier revenue. For GOOGL, use Alphabet’s official investor materials to track Cloud AI, Search/YouTube monetization, capex, depreciation and free cash flow. For META, the July 29 Q2 call should test whether AI ad tools and engagement can absorb elevated infrastructure and talent spending.

This matters for the whole AI hardware chain: if hyperscaler ROI improves, NVDA, AMD, MRVL, TSM, AMKR, and semiconductor ETFs get healthier demand support. If ROI disappoints, supplier strength can still exist, but valuation multiples deserve more caution.

But the workflow should not promote anecdotal lead-time or pricing claims without official confirmation. Prefer liquid US names and ETFs; keep OTC ADRs as signal-only unless liquidity, spread and broker execution are acceptable. Avoid leverage and do not treat first rebounds as automatic momentum restarts.
