3. AVGO has demand proof, but not automatic entry proof

Broadcom's Q3 FY26 release remains a strong primary-source proof point: Q3 AI semiconductor revenue was reported at $16.7B, up 221% year over year and 54% quarter over quarter, with Q4 total revenue guidance around $34.8B. That validates custom AI demand, but the investment question is still valuation, concentration, margin quality, free cash flow, VMware integration and debt service — not simply whether AI demand exists.

4. MRVL and MU remain waiting rooms for stronger proof

MRVL remains the highest-priority custom silicon + optical verification lane. The next planned official event is Marvell Investor Day on 2026-10-06 in New York City, plus delivery against Q3 guidance. MU remains the cleanest US-tradable HBM/memory lane, with fiscal Q4 results scheduled for 2026-09-30 at 2:30 p.m. PT. Both are important, but neither gets upgraded without official margin, FCF and customer-breadth evidence.

5. Buyer ROI is still the firewall for the entire AI supplier stack

GOOGL, MSFT, META and AMZN determine whether AI supplier revenue is durable. For this watchlist, GOOGL remains the clean TPU/COT ROI checkpoint. Cloud operating income, capex, depreciation, leases and purchase obligations matter more than accelerator headlines.

6. AI power chain stays proof-gated

VRT/ETN are cleaner US-listed power-infrastructure expressions; MPWR/ON/ADI/TXN/VSH are component/power-tree monitors. The thesis needs conversion into margins and free cash flow. If backlog rises but working capital worsens or gross margin disappoints, the group should not be promoted.
