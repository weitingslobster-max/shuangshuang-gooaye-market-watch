Theme analysis

1. Enterprise AI workflow: CRM is now a specific proof case

EP692's Salesforce × Anthropic theme is investable through CRM, not Anthropic. The official Claudeforce announcement matters because it places Claude inside governed Salesforce data, workflows, business logic and actions. Salesforce's Q2 FY27 metrics also support the idea that Agentforce/Data 360 is no longer just a narrative. The open question is whether customers pay durable incremental dollars and whether the model layer captures too much value. Upgrade CRM only if AI ARR, cRPO, retention and free cash flow improve together.

2. MRVL / Google XPU / optical interconnect: strongest fresh hardware lane

Marvell's fresh primary-source print gives EP692 a stronger base than a pure supply-chain rumor. The key investment framing is not simply “Google replaces NVIDIA.” It is that hyperscalers are decomposing the AI stack into custom silicon, interconnect, optics, memory and packaging value pools. MRVL can benefit if custom silicon and optical networking scale with margin and FCF. The risk is that the market already prices proof before the quality of that proof is visible.

3. NVIDIA versus ASIC: competition is real, but system control still matters

EP692's NVIDIA stance is balanced: ASICs are real and inference-optimized chips can pressure parts of the GPU TAM, but NVIDIA's training strength, software ecosystem, rack-scale systems, networking, CPU attach and supply-chain procurement still matter. That argues against binary thinking. Keep NVDA as the benchmark; compare every ASIC headline against commercial deployment, TCO, availability, model breadth and customer switching friction.

4. OpenAI Jalapeño / AVGO: bullish demand signal, margin discipline required

OpenAI's Jalapeño posts are primary evidence that first-party inference silicon is moving from concept to measured results. They also reinforce Broadcom's role in custom ASIC/networking infrastructure. But for public-market action, this does not automatically mean AVGO deserves unlimited multiple expansion. The correct gate is whether Broadcom can preserve custom AI margins and backlog quality as hyperscalers modularize designs and demand more control.

5. Optical and substrate bottlenecks: use U.S. proxies, keep Asia local names as signals

EP692's optical and ABF/substrate comments fit the existing state: AI infrastructure bottlenecks may rotate from compute into interconnect, packaging and materials. Under a U.S.-tradable mandate, MRVL, COHR, LITE, NOK, SMH and SOXX are the main tools. Taiwan/Japan local names can validate lead-time and pricing pressure, but they should not appear as direct recommendations in this report.
