Theme analysis

1. EP686 is a risk-control episode, not a crash-call episode

The new Gooaye input frames the recent correction as a test of positioning and risk appetite. The practical portfolio rule is simple: the first groups to reclaim highs deserve research attention, but not automatic capital. For family-level investing, the improvement is to define drawdown and rebalance rules in advance instead of reacting to every high-beta rebound.

2. AI data-center power architecture is the freshest investable lane

The 800V / BBU / PSU discussion is the clearest new industrial thread. Delta and Lite-On are the named Taiwan local signals in the notes, but the US-tradable implementation should stay indirect and liquid:

  • VRT / ETN for data-center electrical infrastructure and power distribution.
  • MPWR, ON, ADI, TXN, VSH for power management, analog/discrete, and component exposure.
  • SMH / SOXX if single-name evidence is too thin.

The research burden is high: directionally plausible power-density upgrades are not the same as near-term EPS. Require official order, margin, product-mix, or lead-time evidence before upgrading from watchlist to portfolio action.

3. PLTR / NET leadership is useful, but valuation is the risk

EP686 treats expensive software leadership as a risk-on signal. That is useful for market tone. It does not remove valuation discipline. AMD’s official Q2 release already improved the hardware/rack-scale proof lane, while PLTR/NET need the same level of primary-source quality: revenue durability, customer concentration, cash-flow quality and guidance.

4. Optical can lead, but Lumentum is the near-term gate

The optical/interconnect lane remains active because Gooaye has repeatedly flagged the group and EP686 notes early rebound behavior. But LITE, COHR, and MRVL are volatile; upgrade only if Lumentum’s FYQ4 and peer read-throughs show AI datacenter demand converting into margin and cash flow, not just revenue excitement.

5. TSMC monthly revenue gate stays open until primary confirmation

TSMC’s official June and Q2 materials still support the AI/HPC anchor thesis, but I did not use secondary July numbers as a state upgrade because the market-watch rule is primary-source first. The next action is to re-check TSMC IR/SEC for July 2026 revenue and update TSM/ETF posture only after primary confirmation.
