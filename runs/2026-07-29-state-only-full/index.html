Theme analysis

1. Mature semiconductor recovery: better, but still needs breadth

TXN is still the cleanest U.S.-listed anchor for the mature analog / embedded recovery lane. TI reported Q2 revenue of $5.46B, net income of $1.98B and EPS of $2.14, with management pointing to broad growth led by industrial, data center and automotive. Its Q3 outlook of $5.65B–$6.15B revenue and EPS of $2.23–$2.57 supports the idea that the trough has improved.

The investment discipline is to require peer confirmation. STM and AMKR are supportive, but the chain still needs ON, ADI, VSH, ASX, and UMC to confirm orders, utilization, margin, pricing/mix, and cash conversion. If the recovery is only restocking, do not upgrade the whole group.

2. OSAT / advanced packaging: AMKR proof improved, but capex matters

AMKR remains the main material improvement from the prior run. Its Q2 release showed strong revenue, margin and Q3 guidance, giving the OSAT / advanced-packaging lane actual operating proof rather than only partnership headlines.

The risk is capital intensity. Better demand is valuable only if it converts into durable margin and free cash flow. For now, the right label is priority research, proof improved, do not chase.

3. AI capex ROI: Meta is the next checkpoint, not a conclusion yet

META reports after market close on 2026-07-29, with the earnings call listed for 1:30 PM PST. Because this run happened before the release, the report does not infer the result. The upgrade case requires AI ad/tool monetization, engagement, and revenue quality to justify capex and expense intensity. The downgrade case is higher capex/depreciation without clear monetization.

GOOGL remains constructive but capex-sensitive. Alphabet’s Q2 release is already in state as a key AI/Cloud proof point, but the next decision still depends on whether cloud/AI revenue, operating income and free cash flow scale together.

4. AMD platform proof: customer language improved, revenue conversion still ahead

AMD’s Microsoft Azure release improves the customer-proof side of the Helios / Venice / MI455X thesis. The key phrases are deployment at scale, Azure AI services, new EPYC Venice VM series, Pensando DPUs, and 2H 2026 Helios shipments.

This is not yet a stock-price blank check. The next proof must be revenue conversion, gross margin, ROCm/software adoption, supply-chain execution, and customer concentration discipline. AMD remains priority research, not an automatic buy.

5. Memory / HBM: strong but cyclical

MU remains the clean U.S.-listed AI memory / HBM expression. Micron’s latest official FYQ3 report remains supportive of the AI-memory lane, but memory is still cyclical. The right framework is: use HBM/server demand and margins as proof, but stay alert to inventory, pricing deceleration, and guidance rollover.

6. Passive / power: wait for Vishay

VSH is the next clean U.S.-listed checkpoint for passive and discrete power recovery. The company is scheduled to release Q2 results before the NYSE open on 2026-08-05 and hold a 9:00 AM ET call. Do not upgrade the passive lane before orders, margins, and free-cash-flow proof arrive.
