Theme analysis

1) AI infrastructure demand: still visible in primary sources

The official evidence remains constructive:

  • TSMC August 2026 consolidated revenue was NT$514.806B, up 53.3% year over year; year-to-date revenue was up 39.3%.
  • NVIDIA Q2 FY2027 revenue was $96.2B, up 106% year over year; Data Center revenue was $89.0B, up 117% year over year, driven by Blackwell Ultra infrastructure.
  • AMD Q2 2026 revenue was $11.5B, up 50% year over year; Data Center revenue was $6.7B, up 107% year over year.
  • Broadcom Q3 FY2026 AI semiconductor revenue was $16.7B, up 221% year over year and 54% quarter over quarter.
  • Marvell Q2 FY2027 revenue was $2.739B, up 37% year over year; data-center revenue growth accelerated to 46% year over year.

Investment implication: the AI infrastructure lane is not broken. The constraint is entry quality and value capture, not evidence of demand.

2) EP698 cost/spec-down risk: keep it as a filter, not a headline trade

EP698's warning is still the right discipline. If AI customers push for cheaper materials, lower-stack HBM, or tighter supplier pricing, the winners will be the companies that preserve margin and cash flow, not every upstream component story.

3) HBM and memory: constructive, but a near-term event gate is coming

Micron's Q3 FY2026 results support the HBM/memory lane, with record revenue, very high margins, strong free cash flow and explicit HBM4E development momentum. The next important gate is Micron fiscal Q4 results on 2026-09-30.

Stance on MU: keep as priority watch. Upgrade only if Q4 confirms HBM pricing, mix, margin and capital discipline. Downgrade if management points to lower-stack substitution, ASP pressure, or oversupply risk.

4) Power rack / electrical infrastructure: good evidence, timing-sensitive stocks

  • Vertiv Q2 2026 net sales grew 24% year over year; adjusted operating margin improved 410 bps to 22.6%; management raised full-year sales and EPS guidance.
  • Eaton Q2 Electrical Americas sales reached a record $4.0B, up 18% organically; rolling twelve-month Electrical Americas orders were up 41% organically and backlog was up 33% year over year.

Investment implication: the data-center power/electrical lane has real official support. Still, VRT is high-beta and can punish late entries; ETN is the lower-beta expression. Treat 800V/rack-scale roadmaps as multi-quarter evidence gates, not instant revenue.

5) Cybersecurity and AI security: measurable, not automatic buys

  • CrowdStrike Q2 FY2027 ARR grew 25% year over year to $5.84B, with $332.8M net new ARR added in the quarter.
  • Palo Alto Networks Q4 FY2026 revenue grew 34% year over year to $3.41B; NGS ARR grew 63% year over year to $9.10B.

Investment implication: AI security/compliance remains a priority research lane. It is no longer just narrative, but valuation and FCF discipline still matter.
