EP691 investment translation: AI workflow is the asset, not the trade

EP691’s core lesson is that AI reduces the cost of becoming conversant in a company or industry, but does not remove the hard parts: judging information quality, reading crowding, identifying whether the market already priced the news, and deciding when not to act.

For this US-market-watch workflow, that means:

1. Social media is radar, not evidence. X / Facebook / communities can surface events early, but any investable claim must go through filings, earnings releases, IR presentations, or directly observable market data. 2. AI is a document and memory assistant. Use it to search 10-Qs, earnings releases, prior reports, and old notes quickly. Do not let it invent confidence when citations are weak. 3. Price/volume and crowding still matter. If everyone gets the same AI-assisted summary, the edge shifts toward timing, sizing, liquidity and patience. 4. Local ticker diary items stay quarantined. 國巨 and 台達電 are not US-tradable primary conclusions here; TSM, ETN/VRT, VSH/MRAAY and ETFs are the only cleaner US expressions depending on theme.

Theme analysis

1. NVDA: proof cleared, but do not extrapolate into unlimited multiple expansion

NVIDIA’s Q2 FY27 release is the strongest primary-source datapoint in this run. Revenue more than doubled year over year, Data Center grew faster than total company revenue, gross margin stayed around 75%, and the Q3 outlook was above the prior run-rate implied by Q2. Importantly, management’s Q3 outlook assumes no Data Center compute revenue from China, so the official guide already embeds a conservative China/export-control posture.

Investment read: this upgrades the near-term fundamental backdrop for TSM, AVGO, MRVL, AMD, MU, ETN/VRT and OSAT names. It does not remove valuation risk. The better action after a large proof event is to monitor whether leadership holds pullbacks and whether breadth improves, not chase the first gap.

2. MRVL / GOOGL: today’s Marvell call must turn Google COT narrative into numbers

Marvell’s Aug. 19 Form 8-K confirms the Google custom-products relationship and a warrant for up to 58.97M shares at $206.58, with most vesting tied to Google purchase/revenue milestones through FY2033. This is strong alignment evidence, but it also creates two investment questions: how much revenue is visible, and how much dilution/capital intensity comes with it?

Investment read: MRVL remains the highest-priority single-name evidence gate today. Upgrade only if Q2 results/call provide credible custom-products/data-center revenue visibility, margin durability and FCF conversion. Downgrade if the thesis remains mostly headline/warrant-driven or if dilution and capex outrun cash generation.

For GOOGL, Q2 Cloud revenue growth and Cloud operating income were very strong, but free cash flow was negative after capex. That is exactly the EP689/EP690 firewall: supplier strength is sustainable only if hyperscaler AI infrastructure produces customer-side economics.

3. AVGO: still strong, but COT pressure becomes a margin-pool test

Broadcom’s latest official results described record revenue, operating profit and free cash flow, driven in part by AI semiconductor growth. EP690’s COT framing should not be read as an automatic AVGO short thesis. The better framing is business-model durability: can Broadcom keep premium custom-ASIC/networking economics while hyperscalers split design, IP, interface, memory and packaging across more vendors?

Investment read: AVGO remains priority research. The next evidence is AI semiconductor revenue, backlog quality, customer concentration and custom ASIC margins. If cash conversion stays excellent, the COT risk is manageable; if margin pools fragment, AVGO’s valuation support weakens.

4. TSM: cleanest liquid anchor for local supply-chain strength

TSMC’s July revenue was NT$467.58B, up 44.7% year over year, and January-July revenue was up 37.0% year over year. This supports the AI/HPC foundry anchor thesis better than most secondary supply-chain chatter.

Investment read: TSM remains the cleanest US-tradable anchor. It should be accumulated only with valuation discipline and staged entry; do not use TSM strength to justify chasing every local-only component name.

5. AI power, OSAT and memory: second-derivative beneficiaries still need cash conversion

Vertiv’s Q2 showed strong organic growth, margin expansion and adjusted FCF, while Eaton’s Q2 showed accelerating Electrical orders and backlog. Amkor’s Q2 and NVIDIA partnership keep advanced packaging/test active, and Micron’s fiscal Q3 keeps HBM/memory scarcity relevant.

Investment read: ETN is the lower-beta infrastructure lane; VRT is the higher-beta lane. AMKR and ASX are proof lanes for packaging/test economics. MU is the HBM valuation-ceiling test. The common rule is margin and FCF conversion, not theme heat.

Valuation and entry discipline

  • Core watch: NVDA, TSM, GOOGL, ETN and diversified semi ETFs.
  • Priority research: MRVL, AVGO, AMD, MU, VRT, AMKR, ASX and PLTR.
  • Small satellite only: thin OTC expressions such as MRAAY only if broker-level liquidity and spread are acceptable.
  • Avoid / do not chase: Taiwan/Japan/Korea local-only tickers, pre-call MRVL momentum, post-NVDA FOMO gaps, and overextended high-beta AI infrastructure/software names.
  • Process rule from EP691: every new social-media idea must pass a three-part checklist: primary-source proof, crowding/price-volume check, and explicit sizing/risk decision.
