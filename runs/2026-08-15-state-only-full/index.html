Theme analysis

1. Cloud landlord thesis: good business quality, still valuation-sensitive

Alphabet remains the key new EP687 idea. The attraction is business quality plus infrastructure ownership: search cash flow, Google Cloud, TPU, YouTube, and data-center scale. The risk is equally clear: if AI competition forces capex faster than monetization, the stock can de-rate even while the company remains excellent.

Action discipline: keep GOOGL/GOOG as priority research / core watch, not a chase. Upgrade only if Google Cloud revenue and operating income continue to scale while management ties capex to customer demand and measurable AI product revenue. Downgrade if capex intensity rises while FCF quality or cloud margin weakens.

2. AI power/thermal: still cleaner than many software narratives

Vertiv and Eaton remain the cleanest US-tradable expressions of data-center physical constraints. The thesis is not simply “AI grows”; it is that power delivery, cooling, UPS, switchgear, and electrical infrastructure are becoming gating items for AI capacity. This lane deserves priority, but high expectations mean valuation discipline matters.

Action discipline: VRT is higher beta; ETN is steadier but less pure. Use order/backlog/margin proof events rather than headline enthusiasm.

3. Optical/interconnect: upside exists, but use satellite sizing

LITE, COHR, and MRVL remain priority monitors because AI clusters need faster optical connectivity and custom silicon networking. The risk is that optical names can look compelling right before inventory, customer concentration, or telecom drag reappears. MRVL has the cleanest large-cap US-listed profile in this lane; LITE/COHR need smaller sizing and stricter FCF/customer-breadth checks.

Action discipline: prioritize proof of AI datacenter revenue durability and cash conversion. Avoid turning optical into a core position without customer diversification evidence.

4. TSM: best liquid anchor, but no geopolitics-driven oversizing

TSM remains the liquid US-tradable anchor for AI silicon manufacturing. The right trigger is not daily geopolitics; it is monthly revenue, gross margin, capex discipline, advanced packaging capacity, and management commentary. If TSM continues to show AI demand strength, it supports the whole AI hardware chain. If revenue momentum weakens, reduce confidence in higher-beta satellites first.

5. Mature/power semis: wait for evidence, not just lower multiples

TXN, NXPI, ADI, ON, STM, and VSH can work if industrial/auto inventories normalize and power/mature-node demand turns. But this lane is less clean than cloud/power/TSM until bookings, utilization, pricing, and margin conversion improve. Lower valuation alone is not enough.
