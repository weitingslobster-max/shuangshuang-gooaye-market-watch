Investment judgment: keep SNPS/CDNS as priority research. SNPS gets the cleaner near-term catalyst because the OpenAI partnership is direct and newly announced. The blocker is valuation: EDA quality can be excellent while stock entry remains unattractive.

2) Jalapeño remains real, but AVGO still needs economic translation

OpenAI and Broadcom’s Jalapeño materials remain the anchor proof for custom inference silicon: OpenAI designed the accelerator, Broadcom is the silicon/networking partner, Celestica participates in systems, and the broader collaboration targets gigawatt-scale deployment. The investment question is now less “is this real?” and more “who captures durable economics?”

Investment judgment: AVGO stays priority research, not an automatic add. Watch customer concentration, custom ASIC gross margin, networking attach, financing/backstop risk, and free-cash-flow conversion.

3) MRVL has a near-term information event — wait for it

Marvell’s official IR calendar lists Investor Day on 2026-10-06 in New York City, and recent Marvell optical-connectivity materials support the technical relevance of 800G/1.6T, 2nm optical DSPs and AI data-center connectivity. But technology relevance is not the same as stock attractiveness.

Investment judgment: keep MRVL as a priority event gate. The report should not front-run the meeting. Upgrade only if management gives concrete evidence on custom silicon revenue durability, optical/DSP ramps, customer breadth, long-term margin and FCF.

4) MU cleared one gate; the next gate is sustainability

The next evaluation should focus on whether FQ1-27 guidance converts and whether management avoids the classic memory-cycle traps: overbuilding, rising inventory, ASP rollover and capex that outruns demand.

Investment judgment: maintain MU as core watch, but do not treat one exceptional print as a permanent margin regime.
